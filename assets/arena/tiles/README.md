# Arena Tile Assets

Extracted from `assets/Bo-gach-nen-san-dau.png`; source image is unchanged. The PNG cutouts keep their original alpha channel and include 6 px transparent padding.

- `floor-*.png`: floor panel variants and the large objective-zone panel. Review `contact-sheet.jpg` to choose variants.
- `wall-straight-h-*.png`, `wall-straight-v-*.png`: straight boundary sections.
- `wall-corner-or-junction-*.png`: corner or joined wall pieces.
- `arena-structure-*.png`: large props and structural pieces.
- `debris-or-vfx-*.png`: debris clusters and impact effects.
- `manifest.json`: original atlas coordinates, crop dimensions, grouping, and extraction settings.

The extracted sprites are source assets for later arena composition; they are not wired into the game renderer yet.
